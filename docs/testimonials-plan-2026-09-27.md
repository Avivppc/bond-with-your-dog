> Hi [Name]! I'm building the new BONDED website and I'd love to feature you and [Dog]. Would you be okay with me using this quote from the group, with your first name, [Dog]'s name and your country?
>

>
> If you have a favourite photo or short clip of the two of you I could show alongside it, even better. Totally fine to say no or to change the wording. Thank you for being part of this from the start ❤️
