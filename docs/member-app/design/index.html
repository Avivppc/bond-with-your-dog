What this lesson is about

Focus isn't a switch. It rises and falls in small waves. You'll learn the four signals that tell you Luna is ready, and the two that mean it's time to stop.

Cue: "Look"Cue: "Break"
Key takeaways

checkSniffing or turning away means pause
checkEnd on a success, not on fatigue

Help

How can we help?

How long should a practice session be?add

Five to ten minutes is ideal. Two short sessions beat one long one. Stop while your dog still wants more.

When do new lessons open?add

A new lesson opens every three days, so each skill has time to settle. You can rewatch any open lesson at any time.

Can I train two dogs on one account?add

Yes. Add each dog under Your dogs. Each one keeps its own progress, plan and feedback.

My dog is a puppy. Is Bonded safe?add

Foundations is gentle, but for puppies under 12 months skip jumps and paws-up moves, and check with your vet first.

How do I film a good video for Roni?add

Film landscape from the side, keep the whole dog in frame and aim for 30 to 90 seconds in daylight.

Where do I manage my purchase?add

Purchases and receipts live on Kajabi. Open Membership & purchases to find the link.
